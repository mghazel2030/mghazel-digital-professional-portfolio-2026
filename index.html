About Me

I am a Senior Computer Vision and Machine Learning Engineer & Research Scientist with more than 20 years of experience designing, developing, and deploying end-to-end machine vision systems for real-world industrial, medical, geospatial, and AI applications.

Born and raised in Tunisia, I earned a scholarship to study Engineering and Mathematics at the University of Arizona before pursuing graduate studies in Canada, where I completed M.A.Sc., Ph.D., and M.Eng. degrees. I am a proud Canadian and I speak English and French fluently.

My work bridges imaging hardware, computer vision, machine learning, deep learning, software engineering, and production deployment, transforming research into reliable engineering solutions.

  • Education: B.A.Sc. (University of Arizona); M.A.Sc. & Ph.D. (University of Waterloo); M.Eng. (University of Ottawa).
  • Industrial inspection: End-to-end machine vision systems, imaging hardware optimization, AI processing, and decision support.
  • Application domains: Manufacturing, automotive, semiconductor QA/QC, remote sensing, medical imaging, retail analytics, agriculture, mining, and wearable technologies.
  • Multimodal imaging: Industrial ISP pipelines, SAR/optical satellite imagery, full-motion video, SEM / TEM, CT / MRI and LiDAR 3D volume data, etc.
  • End-to-end industrial machine vision inspection systems: Automated quality control solution that combines hardware and software to capture, process, and analyze visual data directly on a production line.
  • Professional development: AI Software Development Bootcamp (UBC Extended Learning / Circuit Stream) plus advanced Coursera/Udemy specializations.
